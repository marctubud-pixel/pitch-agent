## F06: WorkBot no longer has to sit on phone content

**What was there.** The launcher (character and bubble) is fixed at the bottom right. Measured before the change with `obstruct-f06.mjs`, scrolling through six pages at 375 and 390px: on the stage and overview pages the launcher passes over 1–8 controls and 8–25 text blocks per page (for example "Approve Visual development and continue", stage chips and the strategy circle buttons). With a dialog open it was already under the backdrop, so it was not clickable there.

**What changed (phones only):**
- **Hide and restore.** A 30px "Hide WorkBot" control on the character's corner docks it to the right edge as a slim tab with its face peeking out. The tab ("Show WorkBot") brings it back. Focus moves between the two, and the choice is kept for the browser session.
- **While typing.** When a page field has focus, the character, the hide control and the tab all step aside, and they come back on blur.
- **While reading.** The idle bubble does not come out while the page is being scrolled, or for 1.5 s after. Status bubbles (answer waiting, error) still show, except while typing or docked; when docked, the tab carries a small dot instead.
- **Dialogs.** While any modal dialog is open (enlarged view, checks drawer), WorkBot is not drawn at all.
- **Conversation.** The panel stays mounted, so hiding never clears the conversation or an unsent question.
- **Spacing.** Unchanged and not increased: pages already reserve 104px at the end, and the pager at the end of a stage page is clear of WorkBot (checked).
- **Desktop.** No hide control or tab, and typing does not hide WorkBot. The one change: WorkBot is no longer drawn behind an open dialog's backdrop.

**What remains (stated, not hidden).** While undocked, the character still passes over content as the page scrolls; after the change the measurement is the same order as before (1–8 controls per page). The fix is that the person can dock it, and it steps aside during input. When docked, at 390px no control passes under the tab while scrolling. At 375px one stage chip ("6 Content review") passes under it at one scroll position. Nothing is under it at the end of a page.

## F05: what happened to a requested change (read-only)

Shown only where the data holds revision records. No empty panels are added. Nothing is inferred beyond the data.

**Visual development: a direction with a previous refined version** (Northline B and C):
- **Request:** the upheld challenge to the earlier version and the requirements it did not meet.
- **Response:** the recorded resolution.
- **Changed:** artwork v1 → v2, with a "See v2" link to the current artwork. It lists the requirement results that differ (for example "Season shown inside the image: not met → met"), and checks added in the new version are marked as added. It also says plainly: *"Only the requirement results and the agents' description of each version are recorded; there is no detailed list of visual changes."*
- **Independent review:** both versions' average, whether each meets the acceptance rule, and their status. C's v1 honestly shows "meets the acceptance rule · Superseded": it was replaced over similarity, not over score.
- **Still open:** what is recorded against v2, or "Nothing recorded as open for v2".
- **Compare v1 and v2:** opens the existing "earlier versions" disclosure with both side by side, sized to the screen. It appears only when a previous version exists, so A and all of Plumtide get no panel.

**Content review.** An index of the passages the review changed, with each recorded reason, linking to the passage (where the earlier wording is struck through). Northline has 2. It states that the review's 4 revised checks are not linked to passages in the data.

**Copy & themes.** Your idea "Back before the lights are on. Just." shows its recorded response and a link, "See where it is used: direction C, copy lines". The link opens C's copy lines with that line marked "Your idea". This match is word for word only. "Kept by you despite reviewer advice" stays separate from "Passed with notes".

**Your own revision request (decision area).** After "Ask for a revision", the stage shows "Your revision request": your comment, and *"Revision results are not connected in this prototype. The stage comes back to you for a decision (simulated)."* along with "No new version recorded yet". When the demo round returns, the decision panel shows:
- the recorded response text ("Revised in response to your comment (simulated — content unchanged in this prototype)");
- "stage version v2" (while F03's line still says "refined artwork v1");
- *"This demonstrates the revision state only; the artwork has not changed."*

Other stages use "the content has not changed". With a connected backend, where the change landed is shown as "not recorded" instead. Nothing is approved by viewing.

## F07: overview and task readability

- **Shortcuts.** "Handle pending tasks (n)" and "View current results (n)" sit at the top of the Overview. They scroll to and focus the existing sections (in-page anchors; no new routes), and each shows only when there is something to go to.
- **Brief.** When the brief is long, the problem tile shows:
  - the objective;
  - the intended output, clamped to four lines;
  - **key constraints**: the first two from the working brief, plus "+n more in the full brief";
  - **still unknown**: linking to the unknowns on the Brief stage.

  "Show the full brief" expands the rest, using the existing labels: output, occasion, scope, objectives, audience, brand, all constraints, deliverables, timeline, budget, open questions. A short brief is shown whole, as before. Loading and error states are worded as such.
- **Counting.**
  - I checked the shared rule in `proposal/needs.ts` (`needsYou`): one item per stage waiting for a decision, per stage being revised, and for an unconfirmed task card, plus **one** item for the whole list of open facts.
  - The big number now reads "2 items still need your confirmation", with its composition: "1 decision to make · 1 list of 3 open facts. Open facts count as one item."
  - The number and its parts come from `needsYou`, the same function as the tab badge and Home. Nothing is hard-coded.
- **Unchanged:** the order (problem → progress → confirmation → produced → ideas, work, decisions), the eight stages and the five areas.

## Data still needed from the backend (no interfaces were changed in this task)

1. **Revision link between request and result.** A revision request should carry its id into the resulting version and review records, so the UI can pair them without matching by time.
2. **Change location and summary per version.** Which artwork or passage changed and a short description of the change. Today visual revisions only have requirement results and an explanation, and the demo's own revision round changes nothing.
3. **Previous content for every superseded version.** Directions v1, for example, is listed as superseded but its content is not kept, so it cannot be compared.
4. **Checks linked to passages.** Content-review checks marked "revised" should reference the passage they changed.
5. **Explicit "content unchanged" or "regenerated" flag** on a revision result, instead of the frontend relying on the service being the prototype.
6. **Idea usage.** Where a contribution was used (direction or slide and field), instead of a word-for-word text match.
7. **Key constraints marked as key.** The brief has no priority on constraints; the summary shows the first two in brief order.
