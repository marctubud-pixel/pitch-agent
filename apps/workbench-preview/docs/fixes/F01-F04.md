## F01: idea drafts belong to their project and stage

**Cause.** One `Contributions` component instance stayed mounted across stage changes, so its `useState` text survived while its `stage` prop changed underneath it.

**Fix.**
- Drafts (open state, idea type, text, borrowing note) are stored per `proposalId::stage` in session storage, so they survive in-app navigation and a reload of the same tab.
- The form is keyed by project and stage, so its destination is fixed when it mounts.
- The form states "Adds to: Visual development · Northline Outdoor …", and the button reads "Add to Visual development".
- Submit uses that same fixed destination.
- Another stage shows "Unsent idea drafts in other stages: Visual development", with a link to it. Nothing is discarded or moved without the person doing it ("Discard draft").
- No backend draft API was added.

**Acceptance: passed.**
- A Visual draft does not appear in Strategy.
- Returning restores the text, the "Reference" type and the borrowing note.
- Strategy keeps its own separate draft.
- Nothing leaks into Plumtide.
- A submitted idea is listed under Visual development and not under Strategy.

## F02: strategy evidence opens the evidence itself

**Cause.** Chips linked to `research?claim=c1`, but the research page only opened the claim drawer for projects without a research board, and nothing scrolled. The link also used the strategy's short reference (`c1`), not the live claim id.

**Fix.**
- Chips now carry their origin (`back=strategy&at=arg-1`) and label.
- Once research data has loaded, the Research stage resolves `c1` to the live claim and opens it in the existing Reviews & checks drawer. That claim panel already shows support status, the claim, its limitations (when any are recorded), source excerpts with publisher and date, and review findings.
- While loading, it says "Finding the evidence for 'Interviews'…". It never treats loading as missing.
- If the claim is not in the current research, it says so, scrolls the notice into view and offers "Open reviews & checks".
- "Back to the reasoning" appears on the page, in the drawer and inside the claim panel (on phones that panel covers the drawer). It returns to the exact argument step, opportunity or circle, scrolled into view and focused.
- Closing the drawer keeps the claim's row marked and focused.
- Case chips also get the way back.

**Acceptance: passed.**
- "Interviews" opens the claim with its interview excerpt.
- Back lands on step 1 of "How we got there".
- A missing claim is reported.

## F03: the decision names its scope, direction and versions

**Cause.** Without a `dir` parameter the Visual stage always opened on the first branch (A, already accepted). The panel did not name any work.

**Fix.**
- **Default selection:** with no direction in the link, a waiting Visual stage opens on the branch whose current refined visual is `awaiting_review` (C, "Pass it on"). A direction the person picks goes into the URL and is kept across rerenders and reloads.
- **Scope:** the panel states what is decided: "The whole Visual development stage · stage version v1". I checked `MockProposalService.decide`: it takes a stage, marks the stage done or `needs_revision`, and records the decision against the stage. The panel says this is not a decision on one direction, and the approve button reads "Approve Visual development and continue". Semantics are unchanged.
- **What waits:** "Direction C · Pass it on · refined artwork v1".
  - Changes: "first refined version, so there is no earlier version to compare". For later versions it says changes are not recorded separately rather than inventing them.
  - Still open: only what the data records (unmet requirements, an average below 4, a challenge that was not rejected). Here that is the partly upheld knot challenge.
- **Viewing without deciding:** "View the pending work" selects C and scrolls to its refined artwork. A note there ("This is the version waiting for your decision") has "Go to the decision" to come back.
- **Versions:** the stage version and the artwork version are labelled separately; the header's "v2" now reads "stage version v2".
- **Unchanged:** no approval step was added, nothing requires scrolling to the bottom, and "Next stage" is still only navigation.
- **Other stages:** a waiting stage other than Visual shows the scope and "View the pending work" (to the stage body). It shows no artwork details, because the data has none.

**Acceptance: passed.**
- Entering from the Overview lands on C with the panel at the top.
- Picking A sticks, including after a reload.
- "View the pending work" shows C's artwork.
- Nothing is approved by viewing.

## F04: packaging views open larger

**Cause.** The view buttons toggled a `big` state that nothing rendered.

**Fix.**
- Each view button opens the existing `Modal` (native dialog) with the view in `ArtFrame` at its own ratio, sized to fit the viewport height without cropping or stretching. The heading is "Front · A · Ink orchard", with "View 1 of 4, at its own proportions".
- Enter or Space opens it (it is a native button); Escape or the close button closes it; focus returns to the view that opened it.
- Works for every supplied view: front, three-quarter, back, gift sleeve, and C's four cans plus its sleeve.
- Thumbnails gained a small enlarge icon. The artwork is unchanged.

**Acceptance: passed.**
- Every view opens at more than twice its thumbnail area, with the frame ratio within 3% of the artwork's.
- On a 390px phone, both the artwork and the close button are fully on screen.

## Not verified

- Only Chromium (Playwright) was used. Safari and Firefox were not tested, nor were real phones.
- No manual screen-reader pass. Roles, names, focus return and Escape are checked by script.
- **F02 error states:** in the demo's Loading and Network error scenarios the whole project view shows its own loading or error state before the stage renders ([loading](./img/F02-loading.png), [error](./img/F02-error.png)). So the in-stage "research could not be loaded" message (with Try again) is implemented but could not be reached in this demo. The in-stage "Finding the evidence…" state was seen briefly under the demo's normal latency.
- Drafts live in session storage, so they don't follow the person to another tab or device. If storage is blocked they last until reload.

## Backend dependencies (recorded, not changed)

1. **Decision target.** `ProposalService.decide(id, stage, decision)` has no artwork, direction or version field, so a decision is recorded against the stage only. Approving one direction's artwork version on its own, or recording which version was approved, needs the backend to accept and store a target (direction + artwork version) on the decision.
2. **Changes between artwork versions.** `RefinedVisual` has no change list. The panel says changes are "not recorded separately" until the backend provides a diff or summary per version.
3. **Stable evidence ids.** Strategy evidence refers to claims by short refs (`c1`), and the frontend maps them to live ids by suffix. A backend should return the live claim id (or a stable reference) in the strategy's evidence refs.
4. **Drafts across devices.** Drafts are deliberately local. Server-side drafts would need an API, which this task excluded.

## Left as is (outside these four fixes)

- The WorkBot character draws above every modal's backdrop. This is how WorkBot already behaves with all modals.

## F01 follow-up: a success no longer clears newer edits

**Issue.** When the person submitted A and edited the form to B before the request finished, the success callback called `clear()` unconditionally and deleted B.

**Fix** (`src/views/project/ProposalArea.tsx`, `src/proposal/drafts.ts`):
- On submit, the form takes a snapshot of the stored draft (idea type, text, borrowing note) for its own project and stage, and sends exactly that snapshot.
- On success, `clearIfUnchanged` reads the stored draft at that moment, not a value from an earlier render.
  - It clears the draft only if all three fields still match the snapshot.
  - Otherwise the newer draft is kept and its form stays open.
  - A draft discarded while the request was in flight stays discarded.
- On failure nothing is cleared.
- No automatic submit or retry was added, and no backend change.

**Verification** (demo data only, isolated dev server on port 5303). A test-only page patch delayed or failed the demo service's `contribute`; the app code was not changed for this. Script: [`race-f01.mjs`](./race-f01.mjs). Fixed code: 18 of 18, in four consecutive runs.
- Submitting A and editing to B in flight: A is saved, exactly one request is sent, and B stays in the open form, unsent, including 2.5 s later.
- With no later edits, a success clears the form.
- Changing the type, or the borrowing note, during the request: the change survives, and the request carried the values as sent.
- Failure: the error is shown, the draft is kept, and nothing is added.
- Isolation is unchanged: Strategy shows no Visual draft, B is restored after navigation and after a reload, nothing leaks into Plumtide, and A is listed under Visual development only.

**The same script against the old code** (a copy with the unconditional `clear()`, on port 5304): 13 of 18. Exactly the five in-flight checks fail: B is lost, so it is restored neither after navigation nor after a reload, and the changed type and changed borrowing note are lost too. So the script detects the bug.

**Test timing note.** One run of an earlier version of the script failed the "clears normally" check. The draft had already been cleared, but a fixed 2.1 s wait checked the list before it refreshed. The script now waits until the patched request has settled.
