**pending/legacy。** pending阻止新的continue/confirm/verify/step/review与COPY原生start/retry；GET和exact pending只读，既有原稿保留。旧无protocol queued/completed不补造marker或原progress，不因execution on/off升级。原job不能换commandId复用；legacy review同artifact不能新review升级。按原job membership及legacy review target检查的native准入覆盖省略admission和借用ID，也覆盖旧target不再current-copy；独立附加反例已证。
